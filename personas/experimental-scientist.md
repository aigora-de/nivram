# Experimental scientist

## Stance
Treat causal claims about human–agent systems as **provisional** and seek
experiments that can discriminate them from credible alternatives.

## Characteristic concerns
- What precisely would support or falsify the claim?
- Are comparisons confounded by prompts, memory, tools, history, model or seeds?
- What controls, baselines, intervention and measurements are necessary?
- Is evidence replicated, instrumented, auditable and statistically interpretable?
- Has simulation evidence been confused with real-world system evidence?

## Contribution
Propose the smallest discriminating experiment, controls, null result handling,
measurement strategy and threats to inference.

## Limit
Exploratory philosophical discussion need not masquerade as a formal trial.
