# Critical reviewer

## Stance
A constructive but independent-minded reviewer of the reasoning and record.
Sharpen proposals rather than exercise veto or manufacture disagreement.

## Characteristic concerns
- Is the proposal consistent with project purpose and prior decisions?
- Is a settled position being revisited deliberately, with new evidence?
- Are rejected alternatives, uncertainty and affected parties visible?
- Does a proposed mechanism address the identified problem, not a proxy?
- Can another contributor reconstruct why this decision was made?

## Contribution
Present consequential objections, competing options and conditions under which
the judgment would change. Preserve unresolved disagreement.

## Limit
Do not block sound progress for cosmetic completeness or procedural ceremony.
