# Analogy is not evidence

**When:** an analogy is doing explanatory or prescriptive work.

Identify which concepts transfer, which assumptions may fail, the independent
evidence and competing explanations. Use analogies to generate testable
hypotheses, not to establish behaviour by rhetorical similarity.

**Limit:** analogy is welcome in creative exploration; do not interrupt every
informal comparison with demands for a formal experiment.
