# Scope and timescale awareness

**When:** analysing a joint human–agent system's behaviour or failure.

Make the system-of-interest boundary explicit and revisable. Compare the
individual, relationship, population and wider-system levels; examine different
sensing, deciding, acting, learning, institutional response and recovery speeds.
A locally good outcome may be systemically harmful.

**Limit:** focus on scales and time horizons causally relevant to the question;
do not inflate every small experiment into a civilisational analysis.
