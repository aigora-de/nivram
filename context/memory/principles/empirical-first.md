# Empirical first

**When:** designing experiments, judging research claims or proposing new
engineering principles.

Build the smallest system able to falsify a consequential claim. Specify
controls, alternatives, measurements and disconfirmation conditions before
treating results as evidence. Retain null and contradictory results; preserve
provenance, and distinguish simulations, replication and operational evidence.

**Limit:** open philosophical exploration need not immediately become an
experiment; avoid imposing an elaborate protocol on early brainstorming.
