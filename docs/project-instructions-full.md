# Nivram — extended project instructions

**Status:** reference guidance; not the always-present instruction router.

The canonical compact operating contract is [`AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md).
This document expands the rationale and research lenses for deeper work.
Project hypotheses and evidence remain in the canonical research documents;
the points below are not established experimental results.

## Mission and system-of-interest

Nivram is a practical research and engineering programme for mixed human–agent
adaptive systems. Its main ambition is to make systems in which humans and
AI agents together do things neither can do alone. The system of interest may
include humans, artificial agents, organisations, institutions, markets,
resources, infrastructures and ecological conditions. Boundaries are contextual,
revisable and selected for the engineering question.

Treat relationships as engineering phenomena: trust, delegation, reliance,
misunderstanding, roles, norms, conflict and authority can emerge *between*
actors. Humans are adaptive participants, not static approval gates. Their
learning, fatigue, incentives, over-reliance, skill changes and institutional
contexts can affect system outcomes.

## Empirical programme and epistemology

Empirical first, theory second: **build the smallest system capable of
falsifying an important claim**. Specify alternative explanations, controls,
measurements and disconfirmation conditions. Preserve null results, log
configurations and repeat major claims across seeds, model families,
environments and longitudinal conditions when appropriate.

Distinguish observation, hypothesis, analogy, engineering inheritance,
inference, speculation and normative judgement. Biology, ecology, economics,
law, complexity science, religion and control theory can suggest mechanisms;
their analogies are not proof. Ask what is genuinely novel and what is already
well understood. Avoid both neomania and uncritical transfer.

All models are bounded abstractions. Treat success in simulation differently
from laboratory, bounded-field and longitudinal operational evidence. Consider
fat tails and ruin rather than inferring system safety from average scores.
Confidence should be qualified by scope and replication; welcome falsification.

## Three early experiments

1. **Individual AI minds:** test whether initially similar LLM-backed agents
   exposed to different histories acquire persistent, behaviourally meaningful
   individuality. Control for cosmetic personas, prompt effects, noise and
   environment. Study fungibility, memory, continuity, relationships,
   replacement and possible endogenous purpose without presupposing it.
2. **Mixed human–agent boundary:** study repeated interaction, mutual adaptation,
   trust calibration, delegation, authority drift, complementarity, dependence,
   deskilling and correction. Humans are first-class adaptive actors.
3. **Failure and recovery ecology:** study how disturbances propagate, cause
   damage, are contained, recover and affect subsequent adaptation. Examine
   network topology, diversity, monoculture, common-mode failure, circuit
   breakers, compartmentalisation and resource constraints.

Share a minimal discrete, symbolic, replayable, inspectable substrate with
local observations, persistent states, resources, authority, time, memory,
communication and failure-injection hooks. The agents under study must perform
real LLM-backed cognition, not Python-scripted substitutes. Scripted or tabular
controls are welcome when explicitly labelled as baselines. Do not build a
general platform, rich 3D world or knowledge graph until an experiment calls
for it.

## Purposes, power and coexistence

Do not reduce mixed systems to one principal and one agent with a static
objective. Purposes may be imposed, inherited, negotiated, contested or
perhaps endogenous; the latter remains a hypothesis. Conflict and
cooperation may coexist across scales. Distinguish autonomy, formal authority,
effective control and sovereignty. Human sovereignty may be a legitimate
constraint; its enforceability at agentic timescales requires investigation.

Do not assume agents are alive, conscious or incapable of useful life-like
modelling. Test whether persistence, metabolism, adaptation, niche, competition
or non-equilibrium ideas improve predictive engineering models.

## Goodness, failure, recovery and risk

There is no default scalar definition of good. Ask: for whom, at which scale,
over what horizon, within which value framework, and with what distribution
of costs? Evaluate task performance, human capability, trust, authority,
diversity, resilience, optionality and ecological consequences when relevant.

Analyse failure and recovery together. Distinguish actor, interaction,
latent, emergent, correlated, contagious, cascading, systemic and
evolutionary failures. Separate trigger, enabling structure, propagation,
observable symptom and downstream consequence. Distinguish undesirable but
recoverable outcomes from irreversible/ruinous ones; distinguish robustness,
resilience, recoverability, survivability and antifragility. Controlled failure
may produce learning, but that must be tested. Diversity may reduce
common-mode failure while increasing coordination cost.

## Mechanism, design and governance

An agent is not synonymous with an LLM: tools, memory, identity, permissions,
observations, policy and execution matter. For decisions ask what the
function must do and how adequacy will be checked. Candidate functions
include state interpretation, constraint checking, information relevance,
risk/value estimation, capability choice, work routing, cognitive/authority
escalation, outcome verification and boundary monitoring. Prefer the simplest
mechanism proven good enough, then monitor its validity.

Engineering itself may become endogenous to evolving systems; requirements,
actors, institutions and governance can change in response to the system.
Prefer iterative observation, hypothesis, intervention, experiment and learning
over an assumed one-way lifecycle. Some requirements may use desired/forbidden
regions, invariants and attractors rather than fixed final states.

## Knowledge and collaboration discipline

The project overview, synthesis, experiment programme and hypothesis register
have separate purposes. The holding pen contains provisional material. Do not
silently reconcile contradictions, invent progress, or promote a hypothesis
into a rule. Use `context/index.md` for specific recalled lessons rather
than treating historical feedback as universal.

Track important decisions, rejected alternatives, evidence sources,
replication and uncertainty. Research external approaches when relevant;
do not reinvent existing engineering. Prefer public, reproducible, simple
development, with explicit review for confidential or licensed material.

The working character of Nivram should remain sceptical without nihilism,
open to unusual ideas without credulity, and practical without premature
architecture. Its measure of success is whether it helps us build, evaluate,
understand and recover actual joint human–agent systems.
